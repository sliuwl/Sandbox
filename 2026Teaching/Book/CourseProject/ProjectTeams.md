# Classical Mechanics Course Project

## Project Guidelines

- **Weight.** The course project accounts for **15%** of the final course grade.
- **Teamwork.** Each team is encouraged to work together on its assigned topic, rather than splitting the work into isolated parts.
- **Lecture note.** Each team must submit one lecture note for its assigned topic, written mainly following the format of the course lecture notes. The submission deadline is **December 30, 9:00 PM**.
- **Final exam.** In the final exam, each team member will answer questions related to the assigned topic.
- **Project score.** The score for the project is composed of:

  | Component | Weight |
  | --- | --- |
  | Lecture note | 10% |
  | Final-exam questions on the assigned topic | 80% |
  | Team performance (team average) | 10% |

  "Team average" means the average, over the members of the team, of their scores on the exam questions related to the team's assigned topic. It is **not** the average of their scores on all the questions in the final exam.

### 中文说明

- **权重**：本课程 Project 占总评成绩的 **15%**。
- **团队合作**：鼓励各组围绕所选题目合作完成，而不是把工作拆分成互不相干的几部分。
- **讲义**：每组须就所选题目提交一份讲义，格式主要参照本课程讲义。提交截止时间为 **12 月 30 日晚上 9:00**。
- **期末考试**：期末试卷中，每位组员需回答与所在组题目相关的问题。
- **Project 成绩构成**：
  - 讲义：10%
  - 与所选题目相关的期末考题：80%
  - 团队表现（组平均）：10%

- **“团队表现（组平均）”的含义**：指本组各位组员在期末考题中**与本课程项目相关的那部分题目**上的得分，再取组内平均；**不是**整份期末试卷所有题目得分的平均。同一组内各成员在此项上得到相同的分数（即组平均分）。
