## Question 1

The **Euler–Lagrange equation** for a Lagrangian $L(q,\dot q,t)$ is

$$
\frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac{\partial L}{\partial \dot q}\right) - \frac{\partial L}{\partial q} = 0.
$$

**Explain the Beltrami identity and prove it.**
