# Glossary: 01FundamentalConcepts

A bilingual glossary of key concepts introduced in Fundamental Concepts, Parts A and B (Chapters 1 and 4 of Taylor's *Classical Mechanics*).

| English | 中文 | Brief explanation |
|---------|------|-------------------|
| **Equation of motion** | 运动方程 | A differential equation that determines how the coordinates of a system evolve in time. |
| **Position vector** | 位置矢量 | A vector $\vec r(t)$ that specifies the location of a particle in space at time $t$. |
| **Velocity** | 速度 | The time derivative of position, $\vec v = d\vec r/dt$. |
| **Acceleration** | 加速度 | The time derivative of velocity, $\vec a = d\vec v/dt = d^2\vec r/dt^2$. |
| **Dot notation** | 点记号 | The shorthand $\dot x = dx/dt$ and $\ddot x = d^2x/dt^2$ for time derivatives. |
| **Circular motion** | 圆周运动 | Motion along a circle of radius $R$ with angular speed $\omega$; acceleration points toward the center. |
| **Mass** | 质量 | A measure of an object's inertia, or resistance to changes in motion. |
| **Inertia** | 惯性 | The tendency of an object to maintain its state of motion unless acted upon by a net force. |
| **Inertial mass** | 惯性质量 | Mass measured by comparing accelerations during mutual interactions. |
| **Gravitational mass** | 引力质量 | Mass measured by the strength of gravitational interaction, e.g., with a balance scale. |
| **Kinetic energy** | 动能 | Energy associated with motion, $T = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$. |
| **Potential energy** | 势能 | Energy associated with position, representing the capacity of conservative forces to do work. |
| **Linear momentum** | 线动量 | $\vec p = m\vec v$; describes how hard it is to stop a moving object. |
| **Angular momentum** | 角动量 | $\vec L = \vec r \times \vec p$; describes rotational motion. |
| **Force** | 力 | The agent that changes an object's momentum, $\vec F = d\vec p/dt$. |
| **Initial conditions** | 初始条件 | The values of position and velocity at the initial time; needed to obtain a unique solution of the equations of motion. |
| **Newtonian framework** | 牛顿力学框架 | A practical set of rules based on identifying forces and applying Newton's second law. |
| **Newton's First Law** | 牛顿第一定律 | A free particle moves with constant velocity in an inertial frame. |
| **Inertial frame** | 惯性参考系 | A reference frame in which a free particle moves with constant velocity. |
| **Newton's Second Law** | 牛顿第二定律 | $\vec F_{\text{net}} = d\vec p/dt$; net force equals rate of change of momentum. |
| **Newton's Third Law** | 牛顿第三定律 | $\vec F_{12} = -\vec F_{21}$; interaction forces are equal and opposite. |
| **Conservation of momentum** | 动量守恒 | The total momentum of an isolated system remains constant. |
| **Weak form of Newton's Third Law** | 牛顿第三定律弱形式 | Action-reaction forces are equal and opposite; guarantees conservation of total linear momentum. |
| **Strong form of Newton's Third Law** | 牛顿第三定律强形式 | Action-reaction forces are equal, opposite, and directed along the line joining the two particles; helps guarantee conservation of angular momentum. |
| **Non-inertial reference frame** | 非惯性参考系 | A reference frame that is accelerating or rotating relative to an inertial frame. |
| **Fictitious force** | 惯性力 / 假想力 | An effective force arising in a non-inertial frame, such as centrifugal or Coriolis force. |
| **Centrifugal force** | 离心力 | A fictitious force in a rotating frame, directed radially outward. |
| **Coriolis force** | 科里奥利力 | A fictitious force in a rotating frame, acting on moving objects. |
| **Electromagnetic interaction** | 电磁相互作用 | Interaction between charged particles mediated by electric and magnetic fields. |
| **Lorentz force** | 洛伦兹力 | $\vec F = q(\vec E + \vec v \times \vec B)$, the force on a charged particle in electromagnetic fields. |
| **Field momentum** | 场动量 | Momentum carried by electromagnetic fields, $\vec p_{\text{field}} = \epsilon_0 \int \vec E \times \vec B \, d^3x$. |
| **Polar coordinates** | 极坐标 | A two-dimensional coordinate system using radius $r$ and angle $\theta$. |
| **Basis vector / unit vector** | 基矢 / 单位矢量 | A vector of unit length used to express other vectors; e.g., $\hat r$ and $\hat\theta$ in polar coordinates. |
| **Radial unit vector** | 径向单位矢量 | $\hat r$ points from the origin to the particle's position. |
| **Angular unit vector** | 角向单位矢量 | $\hat\theta$ is perpendicular to $\hat r$ and points in the direction of increasing $\theta$. |
| **Time-dependent basis vector** | 随时间变化的基矢 | In polar coordinates, $\hat r$ and $\hat\theta$ change direction as the particle moves. |
| **Velocity in polar coordinates** | 极坐标下的速度 | $\vec v = \dot r\hat r + r\dot\theta\hat\theta$. |
| **Acceleration in polar coordinates** | 极坐标下的加速度 | $\vec a = (\ddot r - r\dot\theta^2)\hat r + (r\ddot\theta + 2\dot r\dot\theta)\hat\theta$. |
| **Centripetal acceleration** | 向心加速度 | The $-r\dot\theta^2\hat r$ term directed toward the center of rotation. |
| **Coriolis-like term** | 类科里奥利项 | The $2\dot r\dot\theta\hat\theta$ term in polar-coordinate acceleration. |
| **Simple pendulum** | 单摆 | A mass on a fixed-length string swinging under gravity. |
| **Tension** | 张力 | The pulling force exerted by a string or rod along its length. |
| **Work–kinetic energy theorem** | 功–动能定理 | The change in kinetic energy equals the work done by the net force, $\Delta T = W$. |
| **Line integral** | 线积分 | An integral along a path, $\int_{1}^{2} \mathbf F \cdot d\mathbf r$, used to compute work. |
| **Conservative force** | 保守力 | A force for which work done depends only on endpoints, not on the path taken. |
| **Mechanical energy** | 机械能 | The sum of kinetic and potential energy, $E = T + U$. |
| **Gradient** | 梯度 | The vector derivative operator $\nabla$; for conservative forces, $\mathbf F = -\nabla U$. |
| **Curl** | 旋度 | $\nabla \times \mathbf F$; zero curl (for a position-dependent force) indicates a conservative force. |
| **Nonconservative force** | 非保守力 | A force for which mechanical energy is not conserved; e.g., friction. |
| **Time-dependent potential energy** | 含时势能 | A potential $U(\mathbf r, t)$ that depends explicitly on time, so mechanical energy is not conserved. |
| **One-dimensional system** | 一维系统 | A system whose configuration can be described by a single coordinate. |
| **Linear one-dimensional system** | 线性一维系统 | Motion constrained to a straight line, usually the $x$ axis. |
| **Curvilinear one-dimensional system** | 曲线一维系统 | Motion constrained to a curved path, described by a single parameter such as arc length $s$. |
| **Stable equilibrium** | 稳定平衡 | A minimum of potential energy; small displacements produce a restoring force. |
| **Unstable equilibrium** | 不稳定平衡 | A maximum of potential energy; small displacements produce a force away from equilibrium. |
| **Turning point** | 转折点 | A point where kinetic energy is zero and the particle reverses direction. |
| **Bound motion** | 束缚运动 | Motion confined between two turning points in a potential well. |
| **Separation of variables** | 分离变量 | A technique for solving differential equations by isolating variables on opposite sides of the equation. |
| **Cubic potential** | 三次方势 | A potential of the form $V(x) = m(x^3 - 3x)$ used to illustrate escape and trapping. |
| **Arc length** | 弧长 | Distance measured along a curved path; denoted $s$ for a bead on a wire. |
| **Tangent vector** | 切向矢量 | A unit vector $\hat{\mathbf t}$ pointing along the direction of a curve. |
| **Normal force** | 支持力 / 法向力 | A contact force perpendicular to a surface, constraining motion without doing work along the tangent. |
| **Equilibrium stability** | 平衡稳定性 | Determined by the curvature of the potential energy at an equilibrium point. |
